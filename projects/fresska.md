# Fresska project profile

## Product
fresska.ru; Russian multi-category marketplace in development. Five onboarded sellers, no sales at last confirmed check (2026-10-23). Commission around 10–20%, acquiring 7.5%, CDEK connected, Ozon delivery integration not yet tested. These are historical context, reconfirm before business-critical conclusions.

## Brand constraints
Purple/violet mascot MUST remain unchanged unless the user explicitly authorizes modification. Existing logo/brandbook should be inspected, not reinvented. Interested in premium dark visual identity and typography experiments using Unbounded + Onest. Desktop content container 1440px.

## Catalog contract
Desktop 6-column product grid; tablet 3; mobile 2 at last decision. Catalog cards intentionally have NO Add to cart or quick-buy button; purchase action available on product detail page. Avoid recommending quick-add unless framed as explicit test against this requirement.

## UX concerns
Category tree should be universal for buyers/sellers, not seller-specific. Sample discovery journey: generator → gasoline → 3–5 kW → auto-start. Search/category relevance, faceted navigation, seller trust and product comparison are important.

## Distinctiveness
Wants clearly differentiated experience from Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex Market and Golden Apple. Avoid copying conventions' appearance while preserving behavioral familiarity. Mascot, editorial landing, modular hero, interactive storytelling and carefully scoped motion are candidates, not approved solutions.

## Prioritized questions
Can shopper understand Fresska fast? Can user find an exact item? Does seller and delivery information build trust? Can users evaluate products and complete checkout without surprises? How can brand identity survive high-density listings?

## Constraints
Do not change original Figma frames in audit mode. Preserve mascot and media. Provide evidence-backed critiques, not reflexively high ratings. Do not claim real customer behavior without testing.
