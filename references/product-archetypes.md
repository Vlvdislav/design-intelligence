# Product archetypes

## Marketplace / ecommerce
Search intent, catalog IA, faceted filtering, product-card hierarchy, price/seller/delivery transparency, trust and checkout completion. Prioritize predictable commerce conventions.

## SaaS / productivity
Activation, time-to-value, information architecture, forms, data density, error recovery and permissions.

## Marketing / campaign
Clear promise and CTA, story arc, immersive compositions, brand motion—with accessible fallback and responsive performance.

## Fintech / regulated
Safety, clarity, consent, evidence, error prevention, confirmations and accessibility dominate expressive effects.

## Media / editorial
Reading rhythm, navigation, discovery, content provenance and retention; protect readability.

## Entertainment / games
Character, emotion, dynamic feedback; onboarding and state understanding remain essential.

Use other domains by reasoning from audience, risk and key actions. Do not transfer visual gimmicks blindly.
