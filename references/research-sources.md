# Source and access protocol

Favor direct interface observation, user-research publications and authoritative UX resources. Search contemporaneous material when an interface may have changed. Record access date.

Possible sources: accessible Mobbin previews or authorized MCP, public competitor sites, Baymard public research, Nielsen Norman Group, Awwwards, Lapa Ninja, Behance, Refero and user-provided screens.

Mobbin search tools may include search_screens, search_flows and search_sections; library management may be available if connected. Do not assume free subscription gives API/MCP access. Never bypass gates, fabricate screenshots or claim that a public snippet reveals a whole customer journey.

For design gallery examples, explain their visual relevance but never treat inclusion as proof of usability. When comparing competitors, identify observed screen versions/platforms, not timeless facts.

Maintain a research log with source, URL, query, observation, inference, confidence, applicability, limitation and design test.
