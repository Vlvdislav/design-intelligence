# Signature interaction library — mechanisms, not prefab templates

For every mechanism specify `user intent, gesture, state, feedback, mobile analogue, reduced-motion equivalent, UI affordance, engineering feasibility, fallback`.

1. **Cursor-follow object**: smooth orientation of 3D hero or layered 2.5D image. For showcase/engagement. Never depend on hover for navigation; coarse pointer switches to tilt-free static/drag.
2. **Spatial product orbit**: selected item in focus with depth, parallax, drag/snap; preserve arrows, labels, product list, quick accessibility path. Suitable for 3–10 curated products, NOT entire marketplace.
3. **Material reveal / exploded product**: reveal layers/material/components upon scroll or controlled drag; info remains accessible without motion.
4. **Editorial product rail**: alternating visual tempo/scale; make comparison and price remain readable.
5. **Kinetic type as navigation**: words respond to section progress while preserving heading hierarchy, semantics and focus.
6. **Scroll-linked 3D narrative**: tell product story with pin/scrub and clear exit; avoid scroll-jacking and excessive pinning.
7. **Try-on illusion**: pre-rendered state reveal is not real AI virtual try-on. State what is simulated and what backend would require.
8. **Contextual buy dock**: persistent price/options/CTA near product imagery; prevent covering content on mobile.
9. **Reveal-on-hover details**: add click/focus alternative; no hover-only critical data.
10. **Dynamic comparison canvas**: structured comparisons, optional dramatic transitions, keyboard accessible.

Choose no more than 1 strong signature moment per viewport and 2 subordinate effects. Motion should reduce uncertainty, explain a feature or create a differentiated memory, not merely draw attention.
