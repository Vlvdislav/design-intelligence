# Fresska promo landing — quality regression fixture, NOT a ready design to copy

Scenario: user supplies existing Fresska mascot and asks for an interesting promo page. Generic brand landing with centered mascot + heading + three selling point cards is a **FAIL**. Make three spatially and behaviorally different promo concepts, without copying large Russian marketplaces and without a claim of real conversions.

## Brief signals (verify live source)

- Fresska is a marketplace, not children's entertainment. Mascot can be playful while visual system stays sophisticated and commercial.
- Merchandise is broad (not only fashion); the hero must accommodate several categories without pretending the assortment is known.
- The aim of promotion is first discovery and a clear path to explore catalog, not a long empty corporate intro.
- Prefer high-quality product/scene imagery, original visual rhythm and clear UX over hero paragraphs or random decorations.
- Preserve provided mascot rather than inventing a different one; no childish alphabet font by default.

## Three intentionally different hypothesis directions

**A — A marketplace you can enter**: cinematographic spatial scene, large product objects occupying foreground/midground, mascot as a discreet explorer in a believable visual setting; scroll transitions reveal thematic collections; affordance “Explore collections”, fallback is an editorial category list. Question: does the scene invite exploration or confuse the visitor?

**B — The edit, not the aisle**: high-craft editorial collage: dramatic asymmetry, curated lifestyle photography and real product macro imagery; mascot functions as a consistent small annotation signature, not a giant toy illustration; clear functional category navigation. Question: does curation improve understanding of the assortment?

**C — Product-first, instantly useful**: product gallery with an unusual but comprehensible spatial carousel/split catalog; selection updates price/category and contextual CTA; mascot used as signpost; lower visual load and strong first-click clarity. Question: can a new visitor find a category faster while keeping memorable visual identity?

These **aren't** fixed layouts to regenerate. They are examples of how distinct interaction, density, story and hero art direction must be. Invent a project-specific visual language, image plan and section rhythm before Figma production.

## Required proof to show the user

For each concept: 1440-ish desktop key art, at least the following section, mobile editorial re-layout, named active interaction state and reduced-motion variant; note actual Figma file/frame IDs if created. Show honest conceptual weaknesses. Rejections must list screenshot-based reasons.

## Example rejection

'Violet glow, centered mascot in a circle, white H1 above two buttons, three feature cards underneath' -> **reject**. A change of typography color or rounded corners is not an iteration. Replace with a new structural composition and visual story that makes Fresska distinguishable.
